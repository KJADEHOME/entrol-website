FAQ

Wholesale Pet Carriers and Travel Bags
Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for wholesale pet carriers and travel bags?

MOQ is confirmed by SKU and the level of customisation. Send the item numbers and your estimated quantity and we confirm the per-SKU minimum together with the price.

Can I order a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend sampling before bulk production. Sample lead time depends on design complexity, and we share photos and video before the physical sample ships.

Can you build a carrier to my dimensions and pet-size range?

Yes. Dimensions, pet-size range, ventilation, opening and base support are built to your specification and confirmed before sampling.

Can the carrier meet a specific load rating?

The load requirement and base support are reviewed against the selected design. Send the target rating and we confirm what the construction can be built and tested to.

Can I add my own logo and retail packaging?

Yes. Logo application, woven or printed labels, hang tags and retail packaging are supported, with artwork confirmed before sampling.

How quickly will I receive a quotation?

Quotations are normally returned within 24 hours once the specification, estimated quantity and destination are clear.
