FAQ

Wholesale Pet Bowls and Feeders
Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for wholesale pet bowls and lick mats?

MOQ is confirmed by SKU and the level of customisation. Send the item numbers and your estimated quantity and we confirm the per-SKU minimum together with the price.

Can I order a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend sampling before bulk production, especially where food-contact material is involved. Sample lead time depends on design complexity.

Which materials can I choose for bowls and lick mats?

Stainless steel, ceramic, food-grade silicone and plastic concepts are available, each subject to product-specific material verification for your market.

Can you supply food-contact documentation?

Materials are selected and verified per product. We confirm the test requirements for your destination market before bulk production and supply the documentation available for that SKU.

Can I add my own logo and retail packaging?

Yes. Logo application, hang tags and retail packaging are supported, with artwork confirmed before sampling.

How quickly will I receive a quotation?

Quotations are normally returned within 24 hours once the specification, estimated quantity and destination are clear.
