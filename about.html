Who We Are

Craft, Care,
Connection.

Founded in 2005, Entrol (Weihai Yuanchuang Import & Export Co., Ltd.) is a professional OEM pet products manufacturer and exporter based in Weihai, China —a coastal city renowned for its craft heritage and proximity to major shipping ports.

As a leading pet products factory China-based, we specialize in premium pet products —cat trees, pet apparel, and bedding —and export to retailers, brands, and distributors across Europe, the Americas, Brazil, India, and beyond. Return to homepage to explore our full catalog.

2005

Entrol Founded

Established in Weihai with a 15-person professional team and initial focus on home décor exports.

2013

Pet Division Launched

Expanded into pet products —cat trees and pet bedding —driven by growing global demand from European clients.

2017

Factory Expansion

Own production facility expanded to 1,000 sqm. Self-inspection and QA departments established.

2018

Major Brand Partnerships

Established long-term OEM relationships with EU and US retail brands. 300+ sqm warehouse added.

2019–2020

Global Scaling

Added apparel production line. Expanded sales to Brazil, India, and South-East Asian markets.
