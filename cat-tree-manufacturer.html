FAQ

Cat Tree Manufacturing
Frequently Asked Questions

How many cat tree designs do you manufacture?

We have 85+ cat tree designs in our catalog, ranging from small scratching posts to multi-level cat mansions. All designs can be customized in color, material, and size. We also create fully custom designs from your specifications.

What is the MOQ for cat tree manufacturing?

Standard MOQ is 50 units for existing designs and 200 units for custom OEM designs. For first-time partnerships, we can accommodate test orders as low as 30 units on select models to help you validate your market.

What materials do you use for cat trees?

CARB P2-compliant engineered wood, solid wood, natural sisal rope, jute rope, OEKO-TEX certified plush fabric, and carpet. All materials meet REACH (EU) and CPSIA (US) standards. Material test reports available on request.

Can you manufacture custom cat tree designs?

Yes. Our design team uses AutoCAD and SolidWorks to create custom cat trees from your sketches, 3D files, or reference products. We offer full ODM design service including structural engineering and weight testing.

What is the production capacity for cat trees?

Our Weihai factory produces 3M+ cat trees annually for 200+ brands worldwide. We have 8 production lines with dedicated sections for cutting, assembly, wrapping, and QC. Peak season capacity can be reserved in advance.

Do you provide CE marking for EU market?

Yes. Our cat trees are CE marked and meet EN 71-3 toy safety standards. We provide all necessary documentation for EU customs clearance, including Declaration of Conformity and technical files.

What is the lead time for cat tree production?

Sample: 7-10 days. Mass production: 25-35 days depending on quantity. Shipping: 18-25 days to US West Coast, 25-32 days to US East Coast, 30-35 days to EU via ocean freight. Air freight available for urgent orders.

Can you customize cat tree packaging?

Yes. We offer custom color boxes with your brand artwork, reinforced master cartons for shipping, and retail-ready packaging. Flat-pack options available to reduce shipping costs. FBA-compliant packaging for Amazon sellers.

What quality control do you perform on cat trees?

3-stage QC: (1) raw material inspection on arrival, (2) inline inspection during assembly (stability, weight capacity, finish quality), (3) pre-shipment AQL 2.5 final audit. Weight testing up to 20kg. Photo report provided before shipment.

Do you offer exclusive designs for my brand?

Yes. We can sign exclusive manufacturing agreements for custom designs, ensuring no other brand receives the same cat tree. This protects your product differentiation. Mutual NDAs signed before design discussions.
