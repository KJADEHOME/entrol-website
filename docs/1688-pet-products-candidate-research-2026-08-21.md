## Required verification before publishing a SKU

- Stable detail-page URL and exact supplier identity
- 30-day or 90-day transaction signal and repeat-purchase signal
- Tiered price and MOQ by variant
- Material composition, dimensions and net/gross weight
- Inner pack, master carton and carton dimensions
- Custom logo and packaging options
- Product-specific test reports relevant to the destination market
- Supplier permission or Entrol-owned product photography
- Sample availability and actual sample/production timing
- Freight quotation based on verified carton data

## Website decision

Use the observed directions to establish a broad Dog Toys B2B category page, but do not publish them as Entrol SKUs, inventory, fixed prices or best sellers until the verification checklist is complete.
