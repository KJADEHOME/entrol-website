Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for wholesale cat scratching boards?

MOQ is confirmed by SKU and the level of customisation. Send the item numbers and your estimated quantity and we confirm the per-SKU minimum together with the price.

Can I order a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend sampling before bulk production. Sample lead time depends on design complexity, and we share photos and video before the physical sample ships.

Which scratching board formats can I source?

Flat and inclined cardboard scratchers, sisal-wrapped boards and posts, corner and wall-mounted panels, plus replacement boards and refills. Dimensions are built to your specification.

What materials are available?

Corrugated cardboard, natural sisal rope, plush and carpet covering and engineered board. Material, board density and finish are confirmed per SKU.

Can I add my own logo and retail packaging?

Yes. Logo application, printed sleeves, hang tags and retail packaging are supported, with artwork confirmed before sampling.

How quickly will I receive a quotation?

Quotations are normally returned within 24 hours once the specification, estimated quantity and destination are clear.
