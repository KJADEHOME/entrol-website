FAQ

Wholesale Pet Raincoats
Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for wholesale pet raincoats?

MOQ is confirmed by SKU and the level of customisation. Send the item numbers, size range and estimated quantity and we confirm the per-SKU minimum together with the price.

Can I order a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend sampling before bulk production. Sample lead time depends on design complexity, and we share photos and video before the physical sample ships.

Which raincoat formats can I source?

Transparent PVC and EVA raincoats, hooded ponchos, solid colour and fashion shells, plus camouflage, printed and fleece-lined winter styles.

Can you build hoods, leash openings and reflective trims?

Yes. Hoods, leash openings, adjustable closures and reflective trims are built to your specification and confirmed before sampling.

Can I add my own logo and retail packaging?

Yes. Logo application, woven or printed labels, hang tags and retail packaging are supported, with artwork confirmed before sampling.

How quickly will I receive a quotation?

Quotations are normally returned within 24 hours once the specification, estimated quantity and destination are clear.
