FAQ

Wholesale Pet Apparel
Frequently Asked Questions

What types of pet apparel do you manufacture wholesale?

We manufacture dog sweaters, jackets, hoodies, raincoats, seasonal costumes (Halloween, Christmas), bandanas, and cat apparel. All available in multiple sizes (XS-XXL) and custom designs.

What is the MOQ for wholesale pet apparel?

Standard MOQ is 300 units per design (can be split across sizes). For bulk wholesale orders, MOQ is 100 units per design. Mixed designs in one container are welcome.

What materials do you use for pet apparel?

Cotton, wool, acrylic, polyester, fleece, and organic fabrics. All dyes are azo-free. Fabrics are OEKO-TEX certified. We also offer waterproof materials for raincoats and breathable mesh for summer wear.

Can I order pet apparel with my brand label?

Yes. Private labeling includes woven brand labels, hang tags, custom packaging, and size stickers. We can also print your brand logo directly on garments. No extra cost for branding on orders above 300 units.

What sizes do you offer for pet apparel?

Standard sizes: XS (chest 30-35cm), S (35-40cm), M (40-45cm), L (45-50cm), XL (50-55cm), XXL (55-60cm). Custom sizing available. We provide size charts for your retail customers.

What is the production lead time for pet apparel?

Sample: 7-10 days. Mass production: 20-25 days (faster than hard goods). Shipping: 18-35 days by ocean freight. Total timeline: approximately 6-10 weeks from order to delivery.

Do you offer seasonal collections?

Yes. We design and manufacture seasonal collections: Spring/Summer (lightweight, breathable, UV protection), Fall/Winter (warm, insulated, waterproof), and Holiday collections (Halloween, Christmas, Chinese New Year).

Are your pet apparel materials safe for pets?

All materials are OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified, meaning they are tested for harmful substances. Azo-free dyes prevent skin irritation. We also offer hypoallergenic fabrics for sensitive pets.

Can you create custom pet apparel designs?

Yes. Our design team creates custom patterns, prints, and cuts. We work from your sketches, tech packs, or reference garments. 3D pattern making available for complex designs.

What is the pricing structure for wholesale pet apparel?

Pricing is tiered based on quantity: 300-500 units (standard pricing), 500-1,000 units (10% discount), 1,000-5,000 units (15% discount), 5,000+ units (20% discount). Custom quotes available for mixed containers.
