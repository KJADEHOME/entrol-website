FAQ

Dog Toys OEM
Frequently Asked Questions

What types of dog toys do you manufacture?

We manufacture plush toys, rubber chew toys, rope toys, interactive puzzle toys, fetch balls, squeaky toys, and treat-dispensing toys. All available in custom shapes, colors, and materials. We also create custom molds for unique designs.

What is the MOQ for OEM dog toys?

Standard MOQ is 500 units per design. For first-time partnerships, we can accommodate 300 units. Mixed designs in one order are possible with 200 units per design minimum.

What materials are your dog toys made from?

Natural rubber, TPR (thermoplastic rubber), plush fabric (OEKO-TEX), cotton rope, jute, bamboo fiber, and food-grade silicone. All materials are CPSIA compliant (US) and REACH compliant (EU). We provide material test reports on request.

Are your dog toys safety tested?

Yes. All toys undergo choke hazard testing, tensile strength testing, and toxicology screening. We test to ASTM F963 (US), EN 71-3 (EU), and ISO 8124 standards. CPC certificates available for Amazon compliance.

Can you create custom dog toy designs?

Absolutely. We create custom molds for unique shapes, custom fabric patterns for plush toys, and custom color combinations. Our design team can work from sketches, 3D files, or reference products.

Do you provide private labeling for dog toys?

Yes. Private labeling includes woven brand labels, hang tags, custom packaging, and barcodes. We also offer custom retail-ready packaging design with your brand identity.

What is the production lead time for dog toys?

Sample: 7-10 days. Mass production: 25-30 days. Shipping: 18-35 days by ocean freight (18-25 days US West Coast, 25-32 days US East Coast, 30-35 days EU). Total timeline: approximately 8-12 weeks.

Can you make eco-friendly dog toys?

Yes. We offer eco-friendly materials including natural rubber, organic cotton, recycled plastics, and biodegradable materials. Our sustainable manufacturing practices reduce waste and energy consumption.

Do you manufacture dog toy accessories?

Yes. We also manufacture treat pouches, training dummy toys, fetch sticks, flying discs, and dog toy storage solutions. Custom designs available for all accessory categories.

What packaging options do you offer for dog toys?

Options include poly bags with header cards, blister packs, custom retail boxes, display cartons, and FBA-ready packaging. We can add barcodes (UPC/EAN), FNSKU labels, and suffocation warnings for Amazon FBA.
