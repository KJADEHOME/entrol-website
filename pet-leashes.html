FAQ

Wholesale Dog Leashes and Collars
Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for wholesale dog leashes and collars?

MOQ is confirmed by SKU and the level of customisation. Send the widths, lengths and estimated quantity and we confirm the per-SKU minimum together with the price.

Can I order a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. We recommend sampling before bulk production. Sample lead time depends on design complexity, and we share photos and video before the physical sample ships.

Can you build to my webbing width, length and hardware?

Yes. Webbing width, rope construction, hardware finish, stitch pattern and reflective detailing are all built to your specification.

Can the leash meet a pull-strength requirement?

Pull-strength and colour-fastness options are confirmed before bulk production. Send the requirement and we confirm what the selected construction can be tested to.

Can I build matching collar-and-leash sets with my own packaging?

Yes. Matching sets, step-in and no-pull harness concepts are supported, with logo, hang tag and retail packaging confirmed before sampling.

How quickly will I receive a quotation?

Quotations are normally returned within 24 hours once the specification, estimated quantity and destination are clear.
