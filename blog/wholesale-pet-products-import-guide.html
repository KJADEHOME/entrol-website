Wholesale Pet Products Import Guide: Sourcing from China for Distributors

By Entrol Team • July 3, 2026 • 8 min read

Importing wholesale pet products from China is one of the most profitable business models in the pet industry. As a distributor or retailer, buying factory-direct gives you margins of 40-70% compared to domestic wholesalers. But navigating the import process requires knowledge of MOQ, Incoterms, customs, and logistics. This guide covers everything you need to know.

Why Import Wholesale Pet Products from China?

China manufactures over 60% of the world's pet products. By buying directly from a wholesale pet products supplier in China, you get:

  • Factory-direct pricing: 40-70% lower than domestic wholesale
  • Custom branding: Private label options at no extra cost
  • Product variety: Access to thousands of designs and materials
  • Scale: Produce as your business grows, from 200 units to full containers
  • Quality control: Direct factory relationship means better QC

Understanding MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)

MOQ is the minimum number of units a factory will produce per order. For pet products, typical MOQs are:

  • Cat trees: 50-200 units per design
  • Dog toys: 300-500 units per design
  • Pet apparel: 100-300 units per design
  • Pet bedding: 200-500 units per design

Mixed Container Orders

Most factories allow mixed containers — combining different product categories in one shipment. This is ideal for distributors who want variety without hitting individual MOQs for each product. At Entrol, we offer mixed containers combining cat trees, apparel, bedding, and dog toys in a single order.

Choosing the Right Incoterm

Incoterms define who is responsible for shipping, insurance, and customs. The three most common for pet product imports:

FOB (Free On Board) — Most Common

  • Factory delivers goods to the port (Qingdao or Shanghai)
  • You arrange and pay for ocean freight, insurance, and customs
  • Best for experienced importers with their own freight forwarder
  • Lowest cost if you have logistics expertise

CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight)

  • Factory handles shipping to your destination port
  • Includes insurance during transit
  • You handle customs clearance and inland delivery
  • Good middle-ground option

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) — Easiest

  • Factory handles everything: shipping, customs, duties, delivery
  • Goods arrive at your warehouse — you just unload
  • Higher unit cost but zero logistics headaches
  • Best for first-time importers or small businesses

Customs Documentation You Need

When importing pet products, you will need these documents:

  1. Commercial Invoice: Product descriptions, values, HS codes
  2. Packing List: Carton count, dimensions, weights
  3. Bill of Lading (B/L): Proof of shipment ownership
  4. Certificate of Origin: May qualify for reduced tariffs
  5. Material Test Reports: CPSIA/REACH compliance documentation
  6. Fumigation Certificate: Required for wood packaging

HS Codes for Pet Products

HS (Harmonized System) codes determine your import duty rate. Common pet product codes:

  • 9403.60: Wooden furniture (cat trees) — typically 0-4% duty
  • 9504.90: Toys (dog toys) — typically 0% duty
  • 6117.80: Pet apparel (knitwear) — typically 15-32% duty
  • 9404.90: Pet bedding/cushions — typically 3-6% duty

Common Import Mistakes

  • Wrong HS code: Using a lower-duty code to save money can trigger audits and penalties
  • Under-valuing goods: Customs can seize under-valued shipments
  • Missing documentation: Incomplete paperwork causes delays and storage fees
  • No compliance testing: Products without CPSIA/REACH reports may be refused entry
  • Ignoring wood packaging rules: ISPM-15 heat treatment required for wooden pallets

Shipping Costs and Timelines

Ocean freight is the most cost-effective method for bulk imports:

  • China to US West Coast: 18-25 days, $1,500-$3,000 per container
  • China to US East Coast: 25-32 days, $2,500-$4,500 per container
  • China to EU (Rotterdam): 30-35 days, $2,000-$4,000 per container
  • China to UK (Felixstowe): 28-35 days, $2,200-$4,200 per container

For urgent orders, air freight is available but costs 5-10x more than ocean freight.

Building a Long-Term Supplier Relationship

The key to successful importing is building a long-term relationship with your wholesale pet products supplier. Here is how:

  • Start small: Begin with a test order to validate quality and service
  • Communicate clearly: Detailed specifications prevent misunderstandings
  • Pay on time: Reliable payment history gets you priority production slots
  • Plan ahead: Order 2-3 months before peak season to avoid delays
  • Visit the factory: If possible, visit to build trust and see operations firsthand
  • Give feedback: Constructive feedback helps your factory improve

Conclusion: Start Your Import Journey

Importing wholesale pet products from China can transform your business margins. By understanding MOQ, Incoterms, customs documentation, and building a strong supplier relationship, you can create a reliable supply chain that grows with your business.
